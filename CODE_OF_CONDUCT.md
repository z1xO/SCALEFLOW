# Code of Conduct

ScaleFlow follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

We want this to be a welcoming place to learn and build. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and in code review;
- critique ideas and code, never people;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming;
- accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively;
- respect people's time and privacy.

Not acceptable: harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, sexualised
content, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions - and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them confidentially). Reports are handled promptly
and confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants, in proportion to the behaviour and following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
